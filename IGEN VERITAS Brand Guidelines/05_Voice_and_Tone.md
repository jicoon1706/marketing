# 05 · Voice and Tone

> **Voice** is who we are and never changes. **Tone** is how we adjust for the room.

---

## 5.1 Voice Pillars

| Pillar | Means | Sounds like | Never sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Confident, not arrogant** | We know our craft; we don't dunk on the reader | "Your website can capture leads at 3am. Here's how." | "You're doing it wrong." |
| **Clear and direct — no fluff** | Shortest true sentence wins | "RM 500 setup. RM 150/month. That's it." | "Our comprehensive, industry-leading solution suite…" |
| **Forward-thinking** | Solution-oriented, always points at what's next | "Here's what your business looks like next month." | "AI is the future!!" with no specifics |
| **Honest (VERITAS)** | Real numbers, real screenshots, real limits | "Basic won't do WhatsApp — you'd need Growth." | Overpromising to close a sale |

---

## 5.2 Tone by Platform

| Platform | Tone | Sentence length | Emoji |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Bold, punchy, visual-first | Very short. Fragments fine. | Sparing — max 2, functional only |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional, insightful, thought leadership | Medium. Full sentences. | Rare, none preferred |
| **Website** | Clean, confident, conversion-focused | Short, benefit-led | Never |
| **WhatsApp / Email** | Warm, helpful, responsive | Conversational | Light, natural |
| **Proposals / Docs** | Precise, structured, no hype | Full, formal | Never |
| **Client support** | Calm, accountable, fast | Short, action-first | Light |

## 5.3 Language: English + BM

Default is **English with occasional Bahasa Malaysia** — this is how our audience actually talks and it's a real differentiator against foreign SaaS competitors.

### When to use BM

| Use BM for | Example |
|---|---|
| Emotional hooks and pain points | `Leads masuk. Kau tidur.` |
| CTAs that need urgency | `DM 'INFO' sekarang` |
| Relatable, everyday moments | `Customer tanya, tak ada orang jawab.` |

### When to stay in English

- Technical explanations and feature lists
- Pricing tables and contractual terms
- LinkedIn posts
- Anything a client will forward to their boss

**Mandarin** is offered as a chatbot language in Growth and Pro packages only — it is not used in our own marketing copy.

### Rules

- Never machine-translate. If it doesn't sound like a real Malaysian said it, cut it.
- No rojak mid-word (`your bisnes` ✗). Clean switch points only (`Leads masuk. You tidur.` ✓)
- Keep BM casual-but-clean — no crude slang

## 5.4 The Caption Formula

```
[Bold hook — 1 sentence]

[2–3 lines explaining the value]

[Bullet points of key features/benefits]
```

## 5.5 Hooks That Work

| Type | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Time-stamp | Specific hour + silence | "2:47 AM. One enquiry. Zero replies." |
| Cost of inaction | What they lost | "You didn't lose the sale. You lost the reply time." |
| Contrast | Before vs after | "Same website. 11 more leads." |
| Direct question | Named pain | "How many enquiries did you miss last night?" |
| BM emotional | Short, punchy | "Leads masuk. Kau tidur." |
| Number list | Concrete promise | "5 things your chatbot does while you sleep." |

### Hooks to Avoid

- ✗ "In today's fast-paced digital world…"
- ✗ "Are you ready to revolutionise your business?"
- ✗ "🚀🔥 GAME CHANGER 🔥🚀"
- ✗ Anything starting with "As an AI…" or "We are excited to announce…"

## 5.7 Words We Use / Words We Don't

| Use | Instead of |
|---|---|
| chatbot, AI assistant | "bot solution", "conversational AI platform" |
| setup fee, monthly | "onboarding investment", "subscription tier" |
| leads, enquiries | "prospects in the funnel" |
| we build | "we leverage", "we utilise" |
| fast, under 3 seconds | "lightning-fast", "blazing" |
| helps you | "empowers you to" |
| Here's how it works | "Let's dive in" |

**Banned:** revolutionary · game-changer · cutting-edge (in body copy — it's fine in the tagline only) · synergy · seamless · unlock · supercharge · 10x · guru · ninja

## 5.9 Handling Sensitive Situations

| Situation | Approach |
|---|---|
| Client complaint publicly | Acknowledge fast, move to DM, never argue in comments |
| A bug or outage | State what happened, what we're doing, when it's fixed. No spin. |
| Competitor mentioned | Never disparage. Redirect to what we do. |
| Asked for something outside scope | Say so plainly and price it honestly |
| Asked to discount below the tier | Refer to fixed pricing; adjust scope, not price |

---

## 5.10 Voice Checklist

Before publishing, check:

- [ ] Could a Malaysian SME owner understand this in one read?
- [ ] Is there a specific number, time, or outcome in it?
- [ ] Is every claim something we could actually show?
- [ ] Is there exactly one CTA?
- [ ] Would a competitor's copy read identically if you swapped the logo? (If yes — rewrite.)
- [ ] Any banned words?
