## 1.2 The Name

**IGEN** — the spirit of the new generation. Born in the digital age: curious, adaptive, and constantly pushing boundaries. It reflects energy, innovation, and a mindset that embraces change rather than fears it.

**VERITAS** — Latin for *truth*. In a world where information spreads faster than ever, truth matters more than technology itself. It is the foundation of honesty in communication, integrity in design, and transparency in every solution built.

> **Why this matters for the brand:** the name commits us to two things at once — being *ahead* (IGEN) and being *honest* (VERITAS). Every piece of communication should be able to pass both tests. Bold claim? Fine. Bold claim we can't back up? Off-brand.

### Writing the Name

| Correct | Wrong |
|---|---|
| IGEN VERITAS | Igen Veritas · igenveritas (in body copy) |
| IGEN VERITAS (all caps in logos, headers, first mention) | IgenVeritas · IGEN-VERITAS |
| `igen-veritas.com` (lowercase for the URL) | IGENVERITAS.COM |
| `@igenveritas` (social handle) | — |

## 1.7 Target Customers (ICP)

### Primary ICP
Malaysian SMEs that **already have a website but no AI chatbot on it.** They're paying for traffic they don't convert.

### Secondary Segments
- Solo entrepreneurs & freelancers who want their website working while they sleep
- Business owners losing visitors because no one is available to respond instantly
- Businesses relying on a contact form or a WhatsApp link alone, with no automated lead capture
- Owners who want leads but can't afford a full marketing team

### Their Pain, In Their Words
- "Customer message at 11pm, I only see it next morning — already gone."
- "My website ada, tapi tak ada orang contact."
- "I don't have time to reply everyone."
- "Marketing agency quote me RM5k/month. Cannot."

### What They Care About
Cost certainty · speed of setup · not needing technical skill · proof it actually works · someone local they can call
