## Brand notes

- Slides 1–2 use **Hero Gradient** `#7B67D1 → #4196E6` (brand rule: proof/demo posts).
  Slide 3 uses **Purple Blend** `#7B67D1 → #8A5DCC` (offer/CTA posts).
- Accent word is set in **Dark Navy `#0B0B14`** rather than Violet — Violet on a violet
  gradient has no contrast. Navy is in-palette and keeps the one-accent-word rule.
- Type: Inter, ExtraBold headlines / Medium body. Nothing below 24px.
- All website imagery is the real Website 3 export — no AI-generated or mocked-up UI.
