## 4. Typography

### Font Family
**Primary Font: Inter** (or Poppins as alternative)
- Available free on Google Fonts and Canva

### Type Scale

| Role | Font | Weight | Size (Desktop) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / Hero** | Inter | ExtraBold (800) | 48–72px |
| **Heading (H1)** | Inter | Bold (700) | 32px |
| **Subheading (H2)** | Inter | SemiBold (600) | 24px |
| **Section Header** | Inter | Bold (700) | 20px |
| **Body Text** | Inter | Regular (400) | 16px |
| **Caption / Label** | Inter | Regular (400) | 12–14px |
| **Quote / Italic** | Inter | Italic (400) | 14px |

### Typography Rules
- Headlines: Max 4–6 words per line, broken across 2–3 lines
- **One key word per headline** gets a colour accent (use Violet `#7B67D1` or Blue Mid `#488FE3`)
- Body text always in White `#FFFFFF` on dark backgrounds, or Body Gray `#6B7280` on light
- Never use more than 2 font sizes in a single design element

## 5. Logo Usage

### Logo Components
- **Icon**: Circular orbital ring graphic (blue/purple tones)
- **Wordmark**: "IGEN VERITAS" in ExtraBold uppercase

### Logo Versions
| Version | Background | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Full Colour | Dark/Purple | Default — use on brand-coloured backgrounds |
| White | Dark/Purple | Social media, poster headers |
| Dark | White/Light | Documents, print |

### Logo Spacing
- Always maintain clear space equal to the height of the "I" in IGEN around all sides
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour, or add effects to the logo
- Minimum size: 120px wide for digital use

### Logo Placement on Posters
- Top-left corner on Instagram posts
- Bottom-center with website URL on promotional posters
- Top-center on formal documents

## 6. Design Style (Social Media / Posters)

Inspired by respond.io and WhatChimp aesthetics:

### Layout Principles
- **Headline top** (30–40% of poster) → **Visual/Mockup center** → **Brand bottom**
- Center-aligned text throughout
- Generous whitespace — never fill every corner
- One big idea per poster, max 4–6 words in headline

### Visual Elements
- Smooth gradient backgrounds (no textures or busy patterns)
- Floating glassmorphism cards with soft drop shadows
- Product UI screenshots in floating phone/browser frames
- 3D-style robot or AI mascot illustrations (friendly, not corporate)
- Floating icon accents: WhatsApp, AI brain, charts, calendars

### Poster Colour Rules
- Background: Always use brand gradient (Violet `#7B67D1` → Blue Bright `#4196E6`)
- Headline: White ExtraBold, one accent word in Violet `#7B67D1` or Blue Mid `#488FE3`
- Cards/visuals: Dark glassmorphism (`rgba(255,255,255,0.05)`) with subtle border
