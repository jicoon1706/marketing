## Rationale

- **Pain drives the week opens (Mon)** — CB-026, 029, 033, 036 each open a new week with a sharp pain point. Different angles each week: lead loss, website speed, mobile app gap, then a BM-hook for local resonance in Week 4.
- **Education fills the week mid-points** — 7 education posts covering chatbot flows, web fundamentals, automation stacks, Flutter, UI/UX, and AI agents. This deepens authority without needing testimonials or prices.
- **Template C used once (CB-032)** — the "11PM lead DMs" post is the perfect use of the chat mockup format; it shows the bot in action without claiming client results.
- **Two engagement posts (CB-028, CB-038)** — spaced across the month to drive comments and profile visits, building algorithm signals.
- **Single CTA close (CB-039)** — one clean conversion push at month end in BM/English mix; no pricing, just an invitation to talk.
- **No pricing or testimonials anywhere** — all posts work on education and emotional resonance alone, making the month sustainable even before client results are gathered.
