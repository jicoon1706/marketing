The first digital product a Malaysian SME should invest in — and it's not what most agencies recommend.

Most agencies will tell you to run Facebook Ads first. Some will pitch a website. Others will go straight to social media management.

Here's my perspective after working with Malaysian SMEs across F&B, education, beauty, and services:

The first investment should be a lead capture system — not a traffic system.

Buying traffic without having a system to capture and follow up on leads is like pouring water into a bucket with a hole in it.

The right order:
1. Build a clean, fast website that converts (web presence + credibility)
2. Add a 24/7 AI chatbot to capture leads from that website
3. Set up automated follow-up so no lead goes cold
4. Then invest in traffic — ads, SEO, social — to send people to a system that actually works

Most businesses do this backwards. They spend on ads, have no follow-up system, and wonder why their ROAS is terrible.

Fix the bucket before you fill it.
