## Concept

A real-time screen-record demo showing the full journey of a website visitor interacting with an AI chatbot — from first message to qualified lead captured. No voiceover filler. Just clean, fast visuals with on-screen text callouts. Goal: make viewers feel like they're watching money being captured in real time.

**Core emotion:** *"That's what I need on my website."*

---

## Hook (0:00–0:03)

**On-screen text (bold, white on dark bg):**
> "This is what happens when someone visits your website at 11PM."

**Visual:** Screen goes dark → chatbot widget pulses open on a clean website mockup.

---

## Script & Shot List

### Scene 1 — Visitor Arrives (0:03–0:08)
- **Visual:** Browser tab opens, website loads (clean business homepage)
- **On-screen callout:** "11:47 PM — visitor lands on your site"
- **Action:** Mouse hovers → chatbot bubble appears bottom-right
- **Text overlay:** "No staff. No one online. Just your AI."

---

### Scene 2 — First Message (0:08–0:15)
- **Visual:** Chatbot opens. Visitor types: *"Hi, what's your pricing?"*
- **On-screen callout:** "Visitor sends first message"
- **Action:** Bot responds instantly (< 1 second) with a friendly, structured reply covering packages briefly
- **Text overlay:** "Replied in under 1 second."

---

### Scene 3 — Bot Qualifies the Lead (0:15–0:25)
- **Visual:** Conversation continues — bot asks 2–3 qualifying questions
  - "What type of business do you run?"
  - "Do you currently have a website?"
  - "Are you looking for a chatbot, a new website, or both?"
- **On-screen callout:** "Bot qualifies automatically — no staff needed"
- **Text overlay:** "No awkward forms. Just a real conversation."

---

### Scene 4 — Lead Captured (0:25–0:35)
- **Visual:** Bot asks: *"What's the best way for us to reach you?"* → visitor types name + phone number
- **On-screen callout:** "Lead captured — name, phone, intent."
- **Action:** Cut to Google Sheet row populating in real time (or n8n workflow firing)
- **Text overlay:** "Saved to your CRM. Automatically."

---

### Scene 5 — WhatsApp Follow-Up Fires (0:35–0:42)
- **Visual:** WhatsApp notification pops — automated message sent to the visitor
- **On-screen callout:** "Follow-up sent. While you were asleep."
- **Text overlay:** "Your competitor is still waiting for Monday."

---

### Scene 6 — Closing Hook (0:42–0:50)
- **Visual:** Screen fades to brand dark background (navy `#0b0b14`)
- **On-screen text (large, bold):**
  > "This is running 24/7 on your website."
  > "Or it could be."
- **Text overlay:** "DM us BOT to see it in action."
- **Brand logo:** Bottom center. Tagline: *"Powering the future with intelligent solutions."*
