## Content Mix Targets

Per campaign month, aim for this balance across all posts:

| Content Type | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Pain-point / problem posts | 25% | Awareness & emotional resonance |
| Education / how-it-works | 30% | Trust-building & authority |
| Social proof / results | 15% | Credibility |
| Package / offer reveal | 15% | Consideration |
| Direct CTA / conversion | 15% | Lead generation |
