## Formula 1 — Pain Point Post

**Purpose:** Surface a problem the audience already feels but hasn't named. Creates emotional resonance and stops the scroll.

**Structure:**
```

[Reframe: this isn't their fault, but it CAN be fixed]

[CTA — low commitment, curiosity-driven]

[Hashtags]
```

## Formula 6 — Engagement Post

**Purpose:** Generate comments, shares, and saves. Builds community signals that improve organic reach. Not a direct sales post.

**Structure:**
```
[Relatable observation or question — invites response]

[Short context — 1–2 lines why this matters]

[Poll / question / this-or-that — give them something to respond to]

[Light CTA — "tell us below" / "tag a business owner who needs this"]

[Hashtags — fewer, more niche]
```

## Quick Reference: Hook Styles by Post Type

| Post Type | Hook Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Pain Point | Bold present-tense truth | "Your business closes at 6PM. Your competitor's doesn't." |
| Education | Counterintuitive claim | "5 things an AI chatbot does that your staff cannot." |
| Social Proof | Specific result first | "3 qualified leads before breakfast — real client result." |
| Package Reveal | Reframe the decision | "Stop guessing which chatbot package is right for you." |
| Direct CTA | Soft urgency | "You've been thinking about this for months." |
| Engagement | Relatable question | "How long does it take your business to reply to a website enquiry?" |

---

## Caption Length Guidelines

| Platform | Optimal Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed | 150–300 words | Hook must land in first 2 lines before "more" truncation |
| Instagram Story | 1–2 lines max | Text on visual, no long captions |
| LinkedIn | 200–400 words | Professional depth rewarded; no hashtag spam |
| WhatsApp broadcast | 80–150 words | Conversational, warm opener, single CTA |
