## Feature-by-Feature Breakdown

### Where the conversation starts

respond.io is built for businesses already receiving messages — you connect your WhatsApp Business number, Instagram DMs, and other channels so your team handles everything from one screen.

IGEN VERITAS is built for the moment *before* that — when a visitor is on your website and hasn't decided to reach out yet. Our chatbot greets them, asks what they need, qualifies them, and captures their contact. By the time they're in your WhatsApp, we already know who they are and what they want.

**Bottom line:** respond.io manages conversations. IGEN VERITAS starts them.

### Who operates it

respond.io requires someone on your team to build the automation flows, maintain the inbox, route conversations, and monitor performance. It's a platform — capable, but only as good as the team using it.

IGEN VERITAS is fully managed. We build the chatbot, set up the flows, integrate WhatsApp follow-ups (Growth+), and maintain everything. You don't log into a platform. You just receive qualified leads and bookings.

**Bottom line:** respond.io adds a tool to manage. IGEN VERITAS removes work from your plate.

### Lead capture & follow-up

respond.io captures conversations that come *to* you. If a visitor never clicks your WhatsApp link, they're invisible to respond.io.

IGEN VERITAS intercepts visitors *on your website* before they decide to leave. The chatbot collects their name, number, and intent — then Growth and Pro clients get an automated WhatsApp sequence (Day 1, 3, 7) that follows up without any manual effort.

**Bottom line:** respond.io handles the leads who reach out. IGEN VERITAS recovers the ones who almost didn't.

### Lead scoring

respond.io lets you tag and label contacts. It doesn't natively score leads by intent.

IGEN VERITAS Pro automatically scores every lead as hot, warm, or cold based on how they interact with the chatbot. Hot leads trigger a 15-minute callback prompt so you can close them while intent is highest. All leads sync to Google Sheets as your live CRM.

**Bottom line:** respond.io gives you a contact list. IGEN VERITAS tells you who to call first.

### Reporting

respond.io tracks team-level inbox metrics — message volume, response times, agent performance.

IGEN VERITAS Pro sends a weekly performance dashboard every Monday: leads captured on your website, follow-up rates, conversion tracking. Designed for the business owner, not a data analyst.

**Bottom line:** respond.io reports on your team's inbox. IGEN VERITAS reports on your website's sales funnel.

IGEN VERITAS starts at RM 150/month (Basic) — a complete website chatbot, lead capture flow, and booking system. No DIY required. Setup fee is RM 500–2,000 (one-time), which includes building and deploying the full system.
