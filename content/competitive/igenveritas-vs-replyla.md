## Feature-by-Feature Breakdown

### Lead Generation vs Lead Broadcasting

ReplyLA's power is reach — once you have a list of contacts, you can message all of them at once. This works well for re-engagement or promotional blasts to existing customers.

The challenge: where do the contacts come from? Most SMBs in Malaysia don't have a clean, qualified WhatsApp contact list. They have website visitors who looked and left, Instagram followers who haven't converted, and walk-in customers whose details they forgot to collect.

IGEN VERITAS solves the upstream problem. The chatbot on your website captures every visitor who shows intent — they interact, your chatbot collects their details, and they enter your follow-up pipeline automatically. No lead falls through the cracks because there was nobody to chat with them at 11pm.

**Bottom line:** ReplyLA reaches contacts you already have. IGEN VERITAS builds the list for you.

### WhatsApp Follow-Up

ReplyLA lets you send messages to a list. Timing, content, and sequencing are manual — you decide when to send, what to say, and who gets it.

IGEN VERITAS's Growth and Pro tiers include pre-built, automated follow-up sequences: a Day 1 message (while interest is still fresh), a Day 3 check-in (if they haven't booked), and a Day 7 final nudge (last chance before the lead goes cold). Message copy is written for your service category. The sequences trigger automatically when a new lead enters the system.

You don't write the messages. You don't schedule them. They run.

**Bottom line:** ReplyLA is a broadcast tool you operate manually. IGEN VERITAS's follow-up runs without you.

### Lead Intelligence

ReplyLA has no lead scoring or qualification layer — it sends to the list, and you interpret the results.

IGEN VERITAS Pro assigns a score to every lead — hot, warm, or cold — based on how they engaged with the chatbot. Hot leads (high intent, quick responses, specific service requested) trigger a 15-minute callback alert to the owner. You know exactly who to call right now, without reviewing 50 spreadsheet rows.

**Bottom line:** ReplyLA tells you who you messaged. IGEN VERITAS tells you who is ready to buy.

### Reporting

ReplyLA tracks delivery rates and read rates for your broadcasts — useful for campaign analysis.

IGEN VERITAS Pro delivers a weekly performance dashboard every Monday: how many leads entered the funnel, how many were followed up, conversion rates, and which lead scores are trending. It's built for the business owner checking performance over breakfast, not a marketing analyst.
