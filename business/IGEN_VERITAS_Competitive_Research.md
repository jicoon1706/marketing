## What We're Competing On

**Positioning statement:**
> Your website AI sales chatbot captures the lead, qualifies them, and books the appointment. WhatsApp follows up automatically on Day 1, 3, and 7. You just show up.

**Not just a chatbot — a lead-to-booking funnel.**
The chatbot is the front door. The real value is everything that happens after the chat ends: lead scoring, instant WhatsApp alerts, multi-step follow-up sequences, Google Sheets CRM sync, and weekly performance reports. No competitor in the Malaysian SMB market offers this full pipeline at our price point.

## Competitor 1 — SalesHeroAI

**Website:** salesheroapp.com
**Type:** Done-for-you AI chatbot agency, Malaysia-focused
**Threat level:** 🔴 High — closest rival

### What they offer
- Website + Facebook leads → WhatsApp flow
- Auto lead tagging: hot / warm / cold
- BM + English + Mandarin natively (all tiers)
- Voice AI — confirms delivery, collects feedback, saves ~12 hrs/week
- Image analysis — customer sends photo, gets instant help
- AI-suggested replies from past conversations
- 4.9★ CSAT (published testimonials)
- Structured workflow: incoming messages trigger AI that uses memory + keywords to decide next action

### What they lack vs IGEN
- ❌ No published pricing — must contact them for a quote
- ❌ No D1 / D3 / D7 structured multi-step follow-up sequence
- ❌ No Google Sheets live CRM sync
- ❌ No weekly performance dashboard / report
- ❌ No transparent tier structure
- ❌ Unknown delivery timeline
- ❌ No upsell / bundle AI sequences

### Key battleground
Pricing friction vs follow-up depth. They make prospects book a call just to see a price. IGEN shows RM 150 on a landing page. For SMBs making quick decisions, that alone closes deals before the sales call happens.

> **Note on their follow-up:** SalesHeroAI does have basic follow-up — a gentle reminder if a customer doesn't reply. But it's reactive (reminder-based), not structured like IGEN's D1/D3/D7 sequence. That nuance is worth emphasising in marketing.

## Competitor 3 — respond.io (Platform Risk, Not Direct Rival)

**Website:** respond.io
**Type:** Omnichannel messaging SaaS platform, Malaysia HQ
**Threat level:** 🔵 Platform risk only — monitor, don't fight

### Pricing
| Plan | Monthly | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $79/mo (~RM 370) | 5 users, no automation |
| Growth | $159–199/mo (~RM 745–930) | 1,000 MACs, AI agents, workflows |
| Advanced | $279/mo (~RM 1,300) | AI voice, deeper integrations |

> WhatsApp message fees charged **separately** by Meta on top of subscription.

### Why they're a platform risk
A technically-minded client could pay $79–159/mo and attempt to self-build what IGEN offers. This almost never happens for target SMBs because:

- Steep learning curve — no done-for-you setup
- D1/D3/D7 follow-up not a built-in product
- Meta WhatsApp fees stack on top of subscription
- Salon/F&B owners don't want to learn SaaS tools
- No Google Sheets SMB-friendly CRM output

### What they uniquely have
- WhatsApp Business Calling API (rare BSP feature)
- ISO 27001 certified
- Omnichannel inbox (WhatsApp, IG, FB, email, TikTok)
- Native CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho)

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Publish one case study — now** The Crunch leads with RM 18M in sales. SalesHeroAI shows 4.9★. Even a single booking stat from Sofea Beauty Salon changes the entire credibility picture.

2. **Lead with "automated follow-up system," not "chatbot"** "Chatbots" are commoditised. "D1/D3/D7 lead follow-up system" is not. Reframe all copy — the chatbot is the door, the follow-up is the product.

3. **Keep "live in 6 hours" front and centre** The Crunch moved to 14 days. IGEN is still 3× faster. That's a headline, not a footnote.

4. **Target DIY respond.io users as warm leads** Businesses already paying $99–159/mo for respond.io but struggling to configure it are the warmest possible prospects — sold on automation, just need the done-for-you layer.

5. **Add Facebook / Instagram channel to roadmap** Both real competitors offer it. It's table-stakes for Malaysian SMBs running Facebook Ads.

6. **Plan voice AI as a Pro add-on** Only SalesHeroAI has this locally. Build it with n8n + Twilio Voice or ElevenLabs — closes the biggest feature gap and justifies a higher Pro price point.

7. **Evaluate Pabbly Connect internally** $249 lifetime vs ongoing n8n costs for simpler client automation flows. Could meaningfully reduce the RM 375–470/mo tool overhead as the client base scales.
