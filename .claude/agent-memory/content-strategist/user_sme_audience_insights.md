**Who they are:**
- SME owners aged 28–50, often running F&B, beauty, retail, education, or service businesses
- Solo operators or small teams (1–10 staff)
- Active on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook — less on LinkedIn (but B2B decision-makers are there)
- Budget-conscious but aspirational — want to grow without hiring more staff

**Core pain points:**
- Missing leads after business hours (very strong emotional hook for this market)
- No time to reply to every inquiry manually
- Fear of looking unprofessional when slow to respond
- Have heard of AI/chatbots but think it is expensive or complicated
- Tried Facebook Ads or Boost but no follow-up system to convert the leads

**Language patterns that work:**
- Mix of English and BM in the same sentence — natural code-switching (e.g., "Takkan nak miss lead lagi, kan?")
- Direct and conversational, not corporate
- Numbers and specifics build credibility ("40–60% of leads go cold after 6PM")
- Before/after framing resonates strongly
- "Tanpa hire staff tambahan" (without hiring extra staff) is a powerful qualifier

**Common objections:**
- "Too expensive" — counter with RM500 entry price and ROI framing
- "My business is too small" — counter with solo-entrepreneur use cases
- "I'm not tech-savvy" — counter with done-for-you positioning and behind-the-scenes simplicity
- "I already have a Facebook page" — counter with 24/7 automated response vs manual replies

**What content formats they engage with:**
- Before/after comparisons (swipe carousel)
- Simple numbered lists ("3 reasons your leads go cold")
- Relatable scenario hooks ("It's 11PM. A customer asks about your service. Nobody replies.")
- Short Reels showing chatbot in action (screen recording)
- Proof: screenshots of Google Sheets filling up with leads automatically
