First full content strategy for AI Chatbot services delivered on 2026-04-28.

**Five content pillars defined:**
1. Pain Point — lost leads, after-hours silence, manual follow-up fatigue
2. Education & How-It-Works — demystify AI chatbots for non-technical SME owners
3. Social Proof & Results — client wins, lead capture numbers, booking stats
4. Behind the Scenes / Process — build trust by showing the tech and team
5. Offer & Conversion — packages, pricing transparency, CTAs

**Key differentiation angles:**
- Local-first positioning: BM + English + Mandarin in one bot
- WhatsApp-native (not just web widget) — meets Malaysian SMEs where they already are
- Affordable entry point (RM500 setup) vs enterprise chatbot fear
- Transparency on pricing — rare in Malaysian AI agency market
- Show the automation stack (Botpress + n8n) as proof of depth

**2-week sample calendar produced** covering Instagram (3-4x/week) and LinkedIn (2x/week).

**Why:** User wants to launch AI Chatbot social content and needed a full strategy to act on immediately.

**How to apply:** Use these pillars and differentiation angles as the foundation for all future chatbot-related content briefs. Always check if new post ideas map to one of the five pillars.
