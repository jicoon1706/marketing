**General competitor content patterns observed in Malaysian AI/tech agency space:**
- Most competitors focus on enterprise-level AI or generic "automation" messaging with no local flavour
- Pricing is rarely disclosed publicly — creates friction for SME buyers
- Content is often too technical (jargon-heavy) for the SME audience
- Few competitors speak directly to WhatsApp as the primary channel — they default to web widget demos
- Social proof is generic ("we helped businesses grow") with no specific numbers or industries named
- BM content is rare — most Malaysian AI agencies default to English-only
- Behind-the-scenes content showing actual build tools (n8n, Botpress) is almost nonexistent

**Gaps IGEN VERITAS can own:**
1. **Pricing transparency** — publish packages openly; competitors hide pricing, creating trust gap
2. **WhatsApp-first narrative** — position the chatbot as living inside WhatsApp, not just on a website
3. **BM + English mixed content** — code-switching captions feel authentic to Malaysian SMEs
4. **Tool stack showcase** — show Botpress + n8n dashboards as proof of depth without being intimidating
5. **Industry-specific use cases** — beauty salons, F&B, clinics, tuition centres — niche content outperforms generic
6. **Affordable entry story** — RM500 is a competitive and rarely-stated price point in this space
7. **Founder/team humanisation** — Batu Caves, Selangor origin story is authentic and differentiating vs KL-centric agencies

**Why:** Identified during first content strategy session on 2026-04-28.

**How to apply:** When building content briefs, always default to at least one of these gap-filling angles. Avoid generic "AI is the future" messaging that competitors already saturate.
